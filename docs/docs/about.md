# About

The [vox-wm](https://github.com/DerjenigeUberMensch/vox-wm) project was a small
project made against the now archived [WSWM](https://github.com/DerjenigeUberMensch/WSWM),  
However due to its roots in XCB it is not associated in anyway to the [WSWM](https://github.com/DerjenigeUberMensch/WSWM) project.  
It is however some what started from the [dwm](https://dwm.suckless.org/) though we are straying away from that,
there are still roots from the pre [v2.0.0](https://github.com/DerjenigeUberMensch/vox-wm/releases/tag/v2.0.0) release.

## XCB-TRL

The [XCB-TRL](https://github.com/DerjenigeUberMensch/XCB-TRL) project was closely built and monitored with the vox-wm project,
though as of Jan 9 2025 has temporary seized development, until further notice.

## Development

The [vox-wm](https://github.com/DerjenigeUberMensch/vox-wm) project plans to provide future updates, features, and documentation up until the year 2025 (May be extended). 

However *full* support will not end until the year 2026 (May be extended).
